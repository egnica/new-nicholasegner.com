02 / One connected program

Help people care.
Give them a way to act.
Keep them connected.

The website is the central hub for the rose garden’s stories, restoration updates, and opportunities to get involved. Video, email, and social media work together to bring people to that hub and keep them connected.

01

A place to understand and act.

A website where visitors can discover the garden’s history, follow the restoration, donate, volunteer, and join the supporter list.

02

A reason to care.

Video and visual storytelling that introduce the people, history, and ongoing work behind the garden, giving supporters something meaningful to share.

03

A reason to return.

Email and social media connect people back to new stories, progress updates, and opportunities to support the garden over time.

03 / A new digital home

Bring the rose garden’s story together.

The website becomes the central home for the rose garden’s history, restoration progress, stories, and opportunities to get involved. Every video, email, and social campaign has a place to bring people back to.

  • The garden’s history, restoration plans, and progress updates.
  • One-time and monthly donations through a secure checkout.
  • A content hub for stories, photographs, and videos.
  • Easy email signup with an automated welcome message.
  • Volunteer opportunities and a contact form.
What the website work includes

The proposed program brings together website design and development, a content hub, and email setup, each itemized in the investment section. I will manage website updates and publishing using organization-approved information and materials. We will agree on pages, migration, source materials, revisions, and the approval process before work begins. Additional original content creation is quoted separately.

Donation payments will go to an organization-owned account, with checkout supporting both one-time and recurring gifts. We will define who manages donor questions, receipts, refunds, and records during scoping.

06 / Keep communication moving

A clear rhythm.
A person responsible.

I bring the website, production, and publishing together using the content and email systems I’ve built for this work.

Choose the story.

Agree on the update, available media, and the action we want supporters to take.

Create and review.

I prepare the website update, email, and social content. Your designated contact consolidates feedback.

Publish and maintain.

I publish approved material and keep the website and supporter list maintained.

Proposed monthly rhythm

1 website story
1 supporter email
4 social content pieces

The same supplied update can inform the blog and newsletter. Social content is adapted across agreed platforms where the format is supported.

What I handle

Website design and development, the agreed video production, email setup, adding and editing content, approved publishing, and routine maintenance.

What I need from the organization

One main contact; approved facts, plans, historical materials, and updates; access to participants; account ownership; and timely consolidated feedback.

We will define who creates and supplies ongoing source content and what the fundraising or marketing company handles.

What the monthly allowance covers

Routine upkeep and up to 30 minutes of minor website changes each month. New pages, features, original graphics, extra posting, filming, and additional video editing are separately scoped.
